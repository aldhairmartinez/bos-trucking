About B.O.S.

A real yard on Market Street

B.O.S. Trucking & Site, Inc. runs bulk material delivery, tri-axle dump truck hauling, and secure truck parking and storage out of one yard at 2604 Market St in Fort Myers.

We work with contractors, site crews, homeowners and local businesses across Fort Myers and Lee County. The lot is gated and camera monitored, spaces are assigned, and the same people who answer the phone are the people at the yard.
